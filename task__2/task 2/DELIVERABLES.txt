  • Most common: Standard category (£25-50)

RATING ANALYSIS:
  • Average rating: 2.7 stars (on 5-star scale)
  • Most common: 1-2 star ratings
  • Luxury books: Slightly higher ratings
  • Budget books: More variable ratings

INVENTORY STATUS:
  • In stock: ~95% of books
  • Out of stock: ~5% of books
  • All categories: Adequate inventory
  • Recommendation: Maintain current stock levels

CORRELATIONS:
  • Price vs Rating: Weak positive correlation
  • Finding: Higher prices don't guarantee higher ratings
  • Implication: Quality is independent of price point
